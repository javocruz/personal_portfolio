# Art credits

## Complete home: The Course of Empire

Thomas Cole, *The Course of Empire*, 1836, oil on canvas, New-York Historical Society. All five scans come from Wikimedia Commons. Each file page lists the work as **public domain** (the artist died in 1848 and the work was published before 1931). The originals sit in `art-src/` (not committed) and are baked by `npm run make-cycle`.
